## Checked but NOT downloaded (not free)

- Rosatom 2020 declassified film stills: none of them is on Commons as PD / PD-RU-exempt. The stills used by
  Wikipedia are local non-free files: en.wikipedia `File:Tsar_bomba=eM.png` (credited Rosatom), `File:Tsar_photo11.jpg`
  (cloud from 161 km, crown 65 km), `File:Tsar_Bomba_fireball.jpg`, `File:Tsar_Bomba_possible_primary.jpg` (all "fair use");
  ru.wikipedia `TsarBomba_wolk1.jpg`, `Царь_бомба_отделение_ударной_волны_от_шара.jpg` ("добросовестное использование").
  PD-RU-exempt covers official state documents/symbols, not a ministry documentary film, so it does not apply.
  The film itself (Rosatom YouTube, 20 Aug 2020, "Испытание чистой водородной бомбы мощностью 50 млн тонн") can be
  watched as a reference, but no frames are stored here.
- `File:Tsar_Bomba_Revised.jpg`, `File:Tsar_Bomba.JPG` (bomb casing in the Sarov museum): CC BY-SA 3.0, not PD.
  Viewed only for description: unpainted silver-grey metal body with riveted seams, black nose cap, black tail section
  with large fins (museum RDS-202 body; the nose of the real AN602 differed).
- `File:Tsar_Bomba_échelle_animation.gif` (CC0): an artistic to-scale animation (cloud 64–67 km), not a photograph; skipped
  because its brown "dust" stem is not supported by the sources.
- `File:Ivan_bomb.png`, `File:Tsar_Bomba_Paris.png`: CC BY-SA 3.0, not PD.
