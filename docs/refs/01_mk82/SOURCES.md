# 01 Mk 82: reference image sources

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org, 1280 px thumbnails where available). Each one was checked with `file` and viewed. All are US government works, so they are public domain.

| File | What it shows | Source page | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mk82_explosion_saberstrike18_a.jpg` | A live Mk 82 ground burst at Adazi range, Latvia (Saber Strike 18, 15 June 2018), dropped by a Colorado ANG F-16, at about 0.5–1 s. It shows a dark grey-black plume of soil and smoke about 35–40 m wide and 25–30 m tall (scaled from the 6 m containers at left), spiky radial soil jets, and a low dust skirt. This is the best reference for the "black-grey dirt cloud" stage. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Saber_Strike_18_(4493231).jpg | Public domain (US Air National Guard photo, SMSgt John Rohrer; DVIDS ID 180615-Z-QD622-170) |
| `mk82_explosion_saberstrike18_b.jpg` | The same event series in the first tens of milliseconds. It shows a yellow-orange fireball roughly 15–20 m wide with black soot on one side, white dust at ground level, and a **visible thin shock arc** (refraction/condensation ring) about 2–2.5 fireball radii out. This is the key reference for the flash, fireball and shock-ring stage. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Saber_Strike_18_(4493236).jpg | Public domain (US ANG photo, SMSgt John Rohrer; ID 180615-Z-QD622-189) |
| `mk82_explosion_saberstrike18_c.jpg` | A third frame from the same series, with the plume about 0.3–0.6 s old. It shows a black "crown" of soil jets about 25–30 m wide and 18–20 m tall, a small residual orange flame at the base, and a dust puff at ground level. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Saber_Strike_18_(4493234).jpg | Public domain (US ANG photo, SMSgt John Rohrer; ID 180615-Z-QD622-168) |
| `mk82_bombs_kittyhawk_1972.jpg` | Rows of Mk 82 bombs on USS Kitty Hawk, 1972 (Vietnam). It shows the olive-drab body, the **yellow nose band(s)** (the HE marking) and the ogival nose with fuze well, for the bomb model. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mark_82_bombs_aboard_USS_Kitty_Hawk_(CVA-63),_in_1972.jpg | Public domain (US Navy photo, 1972 cruise book) |

Notes:
- Rejected: `US Navy 050225-N-6060O-038` (a Mk 82 water-surface burst, PD). It was downloaded and viewed, then deleted because it shows a water plume, which is irrelevant for the land range.
- Size estimates in the table are my own rough scaling from objects in the frame (±30%). They are not official measurements.
