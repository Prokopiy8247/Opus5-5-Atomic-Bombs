# 10 Grable (Upshot-Knothole, 25 May 1953) - reference images

All files downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org) on 2026-11-07, verified as real JPEGs and viewed.
All are US federal government works (NNSA / DOE / AEC), marked **Public domain** on Commons.

| File | What it shows | Source page | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `grable_reflected_shock.jpg` (921x696) | Very early fireball (well under 1 s) seen from the side: over-exposed white sphere, dark smoke/dust wrapped around it, and the **ground-reflected shock wave visible as a dome under the fireball**; dust/smoke already lifting from the test-target area. Key reference for "reflected shock + dust" stage. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grable_reflection.jpg (original publication: https://blog.nuclearsecrecy.com/2013/12/06/trouble-airbursts/) | Public domain (US federal government) |
| `grable_mushroom_dust_stem.jpg` (1280x1015, thumb) | Classic colour photo with the M65 280 mm gun in the right foreground: brown-orange cap with glowing orange toroid ring, dark brown dust stem, white/grey billowing dust skirt at ground level, black and white experimental smoke screens, white zig-zag **smoke-rocket trails** used to visualise the shock front. Morning light, blue sky with cirrus. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Upshot-Knothole_GRABLE.jpg | Public domain (US DOE / NNSA) |
| `grable_fireball_ascending.jpg` (1280x1016, thumb) | Same event slightly earlier: fireball still glowing yellow-orange inside the rising cap, bright orange ring, short brown stem, wide white dust skirt, smoke-rocket trails; gun and sandbags in foreground. NNSA caption: "The fireball ascending." | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nuclear_artillery_test_Grable_Event_-_Part_of_Operation_Upshot-Knothole.jpg (NNSA/NSO photo library CIC 0315864) | Public domain (US federal government) |
| `grable_cloud_ice_caps.jpg` (763x942) | Black-and-white photo of the late cloud (minutes after burst): tall column with **three successive ice caps (pileus) and an "ice skirt"** part-way up the column (NNSA caption UK-53-128). Shows the cloud reaching high altitude in a stacked, multi-tier form. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Upshot-Knothole_Grable_007.jpg (NNSA/NSO photo library UK-53-128) | Public domain (NNSA / Nevada Site Office) |

Notes
- A colour-shifted low-resolution video frame (`Upshot-Knothole_Grable_6.JPG`, EG&G film still) was downloaded and then deleted: its strong blue cast would mislead colour work.
- Some Grable photos were historically mislabelled as Plumbbob "Priscilla" (1957); the four above carry Grable captions on Commons / NNSA.
- The two colour photos are taken from the gun position, more than 10 km from ground zero; use them for colour and shape, not for absolute scale.
