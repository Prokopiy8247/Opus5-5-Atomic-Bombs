# Bomb 19 — Soviet Test No. 219 (24 Dec 1962), reference images

No photograph or film of Test 219 has been published (none found on Commons, in Russian or US archives online).
Therefore this folder holds COMPARABLE explosions: US Operation Dominic airdrops of 1962 with almost the same
burst height (3.6–3.7 km vs 3.75 km; yields 7.65–8.3 Mt, about 1/3 of Test 219), plus a NASA image of the
snow-covered test archipelago. The closest analog of all is the Tsar Bomba fireball photo in
`../20_tsar_bomba/tsar_fireball_1961.jpg` (same test range, same design class, burst 4.0 km).
All files were downloaded from upload.wikimedia.org, checked with `file` (real JPEGs, all > 10 KB) and opened visually.

Notes for the artist: the Dominic shots were in the tropics in daylight/dawn; Test 219 happened in the polar night
(Sun 11.5° below the horizon), over snow, in air at about −20 to −30 °C. Take the SHAPES from these photos, not the
lighting or the palm-tree horizon.
