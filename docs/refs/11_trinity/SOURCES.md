# 11 Trinity (16 July 1945) - reference images

All files downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org) on 2026-11-07, verified as real JPEGs and viewed.
All are US federal government works (Manhattan Project / LANL / DOE / NNSA), marked **Public domain** on Commons.

| File | What it shows | Source page | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `trinity_fireball_16ms.jpg` (1280x863, thumb) | Berlyn Brixner / LANL high-speed frame at **0.016 s**: smooth, mottled, slightly flattened hemisphere sitting on a bright band of **lumpy "skirt" matter** at ground level (Mack's "skirt of hot, lumpy matter" ahead of the Mach wave). Black night-time background. LANL caption says the highest point is "about 200 m"; by the published radius-time law the hemisphere is about 220 m wide and about 140 m tall at this time (see bombs_10_11.md). | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trinity_Test_Fireball_16ms.jpg (LANL PA-98-0520) | Public domain (LANL / US government) |
| `trinity_aeby_color.jpg` (1280x1472, thumb) | Jack Aeby's colour photo (the only well-exposed colour still), taken from base camp, roughly 16 km away, a few seconds after detonation: yellow-orange-white, boiling, cauliflower fireball with a short dark stem and dust skirt at ground; the overhead cloud deck is lit **red** by the fireball. Strong red/magenta cast (scene lit only by the fireball before sunrise, plus film/print shift). | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trinity_shot_color.jpg | Public domain (photo by Jack W. Aeby, Los Alamos civilian employee, Manhattan Project) |
| `trinity_cloud_10s.jpg` (1280x1727, thumb) | Black-and-white photo of the fireball/cloud at about **10 s**: large, bright, boiling dome-shaped mass, early stem and wide dust base; the overhead cloud layer is lit from below; second dust cloud at right. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trinity_Test_Mushroom_Cloud_10s_2.jpg | Public domain (US federal government; NARA) |
| `trinity_crater_aerial_28h.jpg` (1280x1017, thumb) | Vertical aerial photo **28 hours** after the shot, with a 100 m scale bar and north arrow. It shows the small bright central bowl (about 80-100 m), the dark radial splash of fused and scoured ground (about 600 m across), the radial roads, Jumbo and its collapsed tower to the NW, and the 100-ton TNT test crater to the SE. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trinity_crater.jpg (NNSA/NSO photo library 99150) | Public domain (US federal government) |
