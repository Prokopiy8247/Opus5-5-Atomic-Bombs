# 17 Ivy Mike: reference images

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org) on 2026-11-07. Each one was checked to be a real JPEG and was viewed.

| File | What it shows | Source page | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mike_color_fireball_cloud_IvyMike2.jpg` (1280x956) | Classic colour photo of the early Mike cloud, taken from an aircraft. A brilliant white, cauliflower-shaped rising fireball/cap sits on a dark red-brown dirty stem, above a deck of low trade cumulus lit orange by the fireball. The sky is deep blue to black because of the exposure. Main colour reference for the first 1-2 minutes. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IvyMike2.jpg (NNSA Nevada Site Office photo library, no. XX-11) | Public domain (US federal government work, DOE/NNSA) |
| `mike_early_fireball_highspeed.jpg` (800x648) | High-speed EG&G camera frame (Charles Wyckoff) of the early hemispherical fireball sitting on the horizon. Mottled, patchy surface; flat base on the sea. The NNSA caption says the fireball "measured 3 1/2 miles in diameter". Reference for the first second (shape and surface texture, not colour). | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ivy_Mike_-_fireball.jpg (NNSA photo library no. IVY-52-07) | Public domain (US federal government work) |
| `mike_late_cloud_overshooting_top.jpg` (1600x1301, original) | Later stage of the Mike cloud, taken from high altitude by Lookout Mountain Laboratory (USAF). A cream-white cauliflower cap spreads into a smooth anvil, with a turret (overshooting top) pushing above it. Smooth lenticular/pileus sheets cut across the stem. Dark blue stratospheric sky. Reference for 5-30 minutes and for the "135,500 ft plume" sighting. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ivy_Mike_overshooting_top.jpg | Public domain (US federal government work, USAF Lookout Mountain Laboratory) |
| `mike_sausage_device.jpg` (1280x933) | Colour photo inside the shot cab. The "Sausage" device stands as a tall bright silver unpainted metal cylinder (left), with large horizontal pipes and scaffolding; a man sits at the lower right for scale. Outer-appearance reference only. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ivy_Mike_Sausage_device.jpg (US DOE, via nuclearweaponarchive.org) | Public domain (US federal government work, DOE) |
