# 09 — "Little Boy" (Hiroshima, 6 Aug 1945) — reference images

| File | What it shows | Source page | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `little_boy_bomb_aec_1960.jpg` | Post-war Little Boy-type casing (unit stencilled "431"), side view: long cylinder with rounded nose, tapering rear section, open box tail with internal cross-bracing, single suspension lug on top, small whip antennas on top of the rear body, side fairings. First Little Boy photo released (declassified 1960). Dark, smooth, semi-gloss paint. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Little_Boy_bomb.jpg (US Atomic Energy Commission) | Public domain (US Government) |
| `little_boy_L11_tinian_pit_1945_nara.jpg` | The actual Hiroshima weapon, unit L-11, on its trailer cradle in the loading pit on Tinian, August 1945, nose toward camera (bomb-bay door of Enola Gay visible top right). Shows dark casing with hand-chalked markings, nose plate with three fittings, box tail. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atombombe_Little_Boy_2.jpg (NARA ARC 519394) | Public domain (US Government) |
| `hiroshima_cloud_caron_enola_gay_nara.jpg` | The genuine Hiroshima mushroom cloud, photographed by tail gunner George R. Caron from Enola Gay minutes after the burst. NARA caption: "smoke billowed 20,000 feet above Hiroshima while smoke from the burst ... had spread over 10,000 feet on the target at the base of the rising column." Shows white cauliflower cap, lumpy stem, dark boiling smoke/dust layer spreading over the city. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atomic_cloud_over_Hiroshima_-_NARA_542192_-_Edit.jpg (NARA 542192) | Public domain (US Government) |
| `hiroshima_firestorm_cloud_from_matsuyama.jpg` | Very tall cloud over Hiroshima photographed from the Matsuyama (Shikoku) direction by the 509th. Photos of this tall cloud were for decades used as "the mushroom cloud"; the Commons file title and the 2016 re-identification (Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum / NYT, cited in Wikipedia "Atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki", notes 155-157) treat it as the FIRESTORM (pyrocumulus) cloud, hours (about 3 h) after the explosion. Use only for the optional late-phase "fires/black rain" scene, NOT for the first-minutes mushroom. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Firestorm_cloud_over_Hiroshima_(from_Matsuyama).jpg (509th Operations Group) | Public domain (US Government) |
