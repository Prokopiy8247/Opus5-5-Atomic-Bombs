# 12 Crossroads "Baker" (25 July 1946) - reference images

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org). Each one was checked with `file` (real JPEG) and opened to confirm what it shows.
There are no genuine colour stills of Baker on Commons. The "in color" / "-colorized" files there are modern colourisations, so they were NOT used. For colours, see the visual-signature notes in docs/research_parts/bombs_12_13.md.

| File | What it shows | Source page | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01_baker_column_wilson_cloud_wide.jpg (1280x669, greyscale) | Classic tower photo from Bikini Island, a few seconds after detonation. The hollow water column (about 600 m wide) stands in the target fleet. The cauliflower crown pokes through the top, and the residual Wilson condensation cloud spreads as a flat sheet at cloud-base height. The dark vertical streak at the right of the column is the "rain shadow" of the battleship Arkansas. The palm beach in the foreground gives scale. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Operation_Crossroads_Baker_(wide).jpg | Public domain (US DoD photo, US Army/US Navy; via Library of Congress) |
| 02_baker_spray_dome_early_ms.jpg (500x360, greyscale) | Earliest phase: the spray dome and column just breaking the surface, a few milliseconds after the 4 ms spray-dome start. A bright glow sits on top of the dome (fireball light seen through the spray), with a sun-like glitter path on the lagoon. Ships on the horizon give scale. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Crossroads_Baker_-_4_ms_after_ignition.jpg | Public domain (Joint Task Force One, US government work) |
| 03_baker_base_surge_nagato.jpg (1280x1024, greyscale) | Column collapse and base surge, about 10-20 s. Dark falling masses of water hang from the column (left), and a brilliant white billowing base surge rolls outward over the lagoon. The battleship Nagato is in the left foreground. Published as plate 28 of "Bombs at Bikini", the official JTF-1 report, 1947. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Crossroads_Baker_Base_Surge.jpg | Public domain (US military photographer, US government work) |
| 04_baker_aerial_wilson_cloud_crown.jpg (800x629, colour-channel greyscale) | Oblique aerial view at about 1-2 s. A smooth white hemispherical Wilson condensation cloud covers the column, and the crown of the column (ragged cauliflower ring) breaks through its top. Below it is a flat white disk on the water: the air-shock "crack" / slick zone. Ships give scale. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Crossroads_baker_explosion.jpg | Public domain (US Army Photographic Signal Corps; DTRA photo library CS-1) |
