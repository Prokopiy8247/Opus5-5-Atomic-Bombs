# 16 RDS-37: reference images

All files were checked after download (real JPEG, opened and viewed). Only public-domain or CC0 material is kept.

No public-domain or CC0 photograph of the RDS-37 explosion or bomb exists on Wikimedia Commons:
- `File:RDS 37.jpg` and `File:RDS-37(konstr).jpg` are user drawings under CC BY-SA 4.0. They are not allowed by the project rule, and the second is a construction drawing, which is out of scope.
- The well-known colour still of the RDS-37 mushroom cloud (atomicarchive.com, en.wikipedia `File:RDS_37_mushroom_cloud.jpg`) is non-free (used on Wikipedia under fair use). It was viewed for research only and NOT saved here. Page: https://www.atomicarchive.com/media/photographs/testing/soviet/rds-37-1.html . What it shows is described in `docs/research_parts/bombs_14_16.md` (orange-lit flat cap, thin dark twisted dust stem, wide low dust base, translucent bell-shaped condensation "skirt" around the middle of the stem).

The folder therefore holds a US analog of the same class (an air-dropped megaton thermonuclear airburst at a similar height) plus the carrier aircraft type.
