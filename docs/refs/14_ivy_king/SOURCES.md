# 14 Ivy King: reference images

All files were checked after download (real JPEG, opened and viewed). Only public-domain or CC0 material is kept.

| File | What it shows | Source page | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ivy_king_mushroom_cloud.jpg` (1280x1025) | Colour aerial photo of the King burst about 1 minute after detonation: an orange-white fireball/cap with a dark-brown top, a thin reddish stem, and a large circular disturbance (shock/condensation ring) in the trade-wind cumulus deck around the burst. Thin smoke-rocket trails are visible to the right of the stem base. NNSA Nevada Site Office photo XX-42. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ivy_King_-_mushroom_cloud.jpg | Public domain (US Government work, NNSA/NSO) |
| `ivy_king_distance.jpg` (800x640) | The King cloud seen from the atoll at sea level (NNSA photo IVY-52-10), with observers in silhouette. The strong orange/magenta cast is most likely a colour shift in the old print, because the shot was at 11:30 local time (midday). Use it for composition and scale (a cap far wider than it is tall, above low cumulus), not for colour. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ivy_King_-_distance.jpg | Public domain (PD-USGov-DOE) |
| `ivy_king_film_frames_2s.jpg` (1320x720) | 20 frames taken every 2 s from the US DOE film clip "Ivy King test" (aerial camera, about 43–83 s into the clip). Sequence: white-out, then a hemispherical white fireball with yellow light on the cloud deck, then a flattened white disk (the fireball with lit condensation layers), then a smaller orange fireball inside a dark-brown cap rising on a stem, then a brown-grey mushroom. The film is edited, so do not read real-time intervals from it. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ivy_King_test.ogv (DOE film, nv.doe.gov film ID 82) | Public domain (US DOE). The frames are a derived still made with ffmpeg. |
| `mk6_casing_usafm_cc0.jpg` (1280x923) | Mark 6 nuclear bomb casing at the National Museum of the USAF: olive-drab body with circumferential ribs, a yellow nose cap, yellow bands with red rectangles, and a box tail. The Mk 18 used for King was a high-yield variant built on the Mk 6 design, and its listed outer size (128 x 60 in) matches the Mk 6 (128 x 61 in). This is the best available stand-in for the bomb's exterior. No photo of the actual King test unit is publicly available. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mark_VI_USAFM.jpg | CC0 1.0 (photographer Wilson44691) |

Rejected or not used:

- `Mark_6_bomb.jpg` (DOE/DoD, PD): downloaded and viewed, then dropped as a near-duplicate of the Mk 6 exterior.
