# 13 Storax / Plowshare "Sedan" (6 July 1962) - reference images

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org). Each one was checked with `file` (real JPEG) and opened to confirm what it shows.
Files 02 and 03 are frames from the DOE's 1962 colour cine film. The film stock has faded, so they carry a strong magenta/pink cast. Use them for SHAPE and for the orange glow of the vented gas, NOT for the overall palette. For the palette, use file 01, a properly balanced colour photo: blue sky, tan desert, grey-brown dust, cream-white ejecta streamers.

| 01_sedan_dome_venting_nnsa_xx13.jpg (1280x1013, colour) | Seconds after venting (estimated 5-15 s). A broad dark grey-brown dust column rises from the broken mound. Dozens of cream-white ballistic ejecta "streamers" (clods trailing dust) arc outward and down like a fountain, and a low dust skirt lies on the ground. Clear blue sky, tan desert alluvium in the foreground, distant hills. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Storax_Sedan_nuke.jpg | Public domain (US federal government; NNSA Nevada Site Office photo library XX-13) |
| 02_sedan_dome_bursting_venting_glow_doe_hd10183.jpg (1280x917, faded colour film frame) | About 3-4 s. The mound has burst into a spiky "bush" of ejecta. Through the cracks at its top, orange/salmon-pink glowing gas shows: the incandescent cavity gases venting. Desert flat in the foreground, mountains behind. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HD.10.183_(13387732863).jpg | Public domain (US Department of Energy, energy.gov Flickr HD.10.183) |
| 03_sedan_venting_incandescent_closeup_doe_hd10187.jpg (1280x931, faded colour film frame) | Close-up of the venting: bright peach-orange incandescent gas bursting up through dark falling ejecta curtains. | 04_sedan_crater_nnsa_nf12187.jpg (1280x1016, colour) | The finished crater from the air. A smooth conical funnel of pale tan alluvium, half in shadow, with a hummocky ejecta lip and radial ejecta "dunes" around it.
