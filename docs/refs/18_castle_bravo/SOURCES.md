# 18 Castle Bravo: reference images

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org) on 2026-11-07. Each one was checked to be a real JPEG and was viewed.

| File | What it shows | Source page | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bravo_condensation_rings_red_sky.jpg` (1280x672) | Bravo in the first minute (around 30-60 s). A bright yellow-white fireball is surrounded by several flat, stacked condensation rings/discs, above and below it. The sky and the trade-cumulus deck are lit deep red-orange (pre-dawn). Main reference for the "condensation rings / crimson sky" look. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Castle_Bravo_nuclear_test.jpg (credited to NOAA / US DOE) | Public domain (US federal government work) |
| `bravo_fireball_rings_rocket_trails.jpg` (1280x724) | Close view of the rising, still-glowing fireball (around 45-60 s). Its boiling, cauliflower surface is bright yellow-white below and orange above. It is cut by two thin flat condensation rings. Thin smoke-rocket trails are visible at the left, and the cumulus deck at the bottom is lit orange. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Castle_Bravo_005.jpg (US government photo; noise-reduced copy from sonicbomb.com) | Public domain (US federal government work) |
| `bravo_rising_cap_ice_caps.jpg` (1280x962) | Later in the first minutes. A glowing domed cap with layered "ice cap" sheets and a wide flat disc spreading at its base; a light column below. The whole sky and the cumulus field are tinted red-orange. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Castle_Bravo_Blast.jpg (US DOE) | Public domain (US federal government work, DOE) |
| `bravo_shrimp_device_in_cab.jpg` (1280x1024, greyscale) | The SHRIMP device in its concrete shot cab: a plain, unpainted, light-metal horizontal cylinder with rounded ends on steel stands, with dark cables/pipes attached. Outer-appearance reference only. | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Castle_Bravo_Shrimp_Device_002.jpg (Los Alamos/DTRIAC photo IM-4: D-92-1; higher-resolution copy obtained by FOIA, A. Wellerstein) | Public domain (US federal government work) |
