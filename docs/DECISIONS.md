# DECISIONS

Autonomous decisions taken during development (what, why).

- **D001 (stage 0):** `docs/00_TASK_PROMPT.md` already existed and is byte-identical in size to `PROMPT_atomic_bombs.md` (32151 bytes); kept as is.
- **D002 (stage 1):** Research is run as a parallel multi-agent workflow (8 bomb groups + 1 techniques agent), each agent writes `docs/research_parts/*.md` and downloads 2-4 public-domain reference images into `docs/refs/NN_name/`. A merge agent consolidates into `docs/RESEARCH_NOTES.md` and corrects `docs/01_BOMBS_REFERENCE.md`. Development of the skeleton proceeds in parallel in the main thread to save wall-clock time.
- **D003 (stage 3):** Single 32-bit reversed-Z camera with near 0.05 m / far 420 km instead of two cameras: reversed-Z float depth gives enough precision for both the bunker interior and the 200 km range (no z-fighting observed in tests).
- **D004 (stage 3):** Terrain is an addon-free geometry clipmap (14 levels x 128^2, geomorphed, coarser levels discard pixels covered by finer ones) plus a 6-level high-resolution patch centred on ground zero so craters/domes/scorch are resolved in geometry at 100+ km. Height = shared hash-based value-noise function implemented identically in GDScript (TerrainMath) and GLSL (terrain_common.gdshaderinc). Earth curvature (R_eff = 8500 km, standard refraction) is applied to the terrain and therefore to everything placed on it.
- **D005 (stage 5):** All explosion layers are closed-form functions of the explosion age (raymarched SDF volume + noise, analytic debris on a MultiMesh, shells, terrain deformation via global shader parameters). GPUParticles3D are used only where scrubbing is irrelevant (rocket smoke trail, bunker dust). This makes slow motion, pause, scrubbing and offline rendering deterministic.
- **D006 (stage 5):** Eye adaptation is a script model (not Godot's image-based auto exposure): a camera-metering average of the flash irradiance at the observer (G&D Pmax = 3.18 W^0.56 kt/s, clear-air transmission) and the fireball's own luminance weighted by its share of the view; fast down (0.1 s), slow recovery (4.5 s), capped at 7.5 stops. Deterministic for scrubbing (integrated in 20 ms steps).
- **D007 (stage 4):** Yield ordering. The official records give Little Boy 15 kt and Grable 15 kt (a tie), which breaks the "strictly ascending yield" rule. Little Boy uses the RERF DS02 dosimetry value (16 kt, the current official reconstruction) and is placed AFTER Grable (#9 Grable 15 kt, #10 Little Boy 16 kt). Baker uses the official 23 kt (DASA 1251), Trinity the official 21 kt, so Trinity < Baker. No bomb was replaced: the research agents found every bomb sufficiently documented (see RESEARCH_NOTES.md).
- **D008 (stage 4):** Physics formulas were corrected from docs/02 to the research results (docs/research_parts/techniques_and_physics.md): t_min = 3.5 W^0.4 ms; t2 = 0.0417 W^0.44 s; pulse P/Pmax = (15/7)tau^2/(1+(8/7)tau^3.75); contact fireball 79 m W^0.4 with (W/1 Mt)^-0.03 roll-off above 1 Mt; pressure-dependent surface-burst radius factors (0.55 at 1 psi .. 0.90 at 100 psi); DNA far-field tail below 1 psi; DNA time of arrival (2W for surface bursts); Kingery-Bulmash scaling for HE (x0.82 radii); clear-air transmission exp(-k d_km^0.44); Miller cloud top/cap (x0.82 at dry sites) with measured per-bomb overrides; cloud rise tanh(t/tau_r). Per-bomb measured values (fireball, cloud top, cap, crater) from data/research_values.json override the formulas in bombs.json.
- **D009 (stage 5):** Godot 4 folds the camera exposure multiplier into lit shading, but not into `unshaded` material output or custom `FOG` colours. All custom unshaded effects (explosion volume, dust wall, Wilson shell, shock condensation) and the shared aerial-perspective colour therefore multiply by a global `g_exposure` that EnvironmentCtl updates every frame. (Found by debugging white-clipped fireballs at very low exposure.)
- **D010 (stage 5):** Spherical Wilson condensation shells are limited to yields <= 150 kt; megaton clouds use condensation rings/skirts/ice caps instead (Bravo, Mike), matching the reference films.
- **D011 (stage 6):** Sedan's recommended distance is 12 km instead of the 35 km first proposed: the 104 kt yield is buried at 194 m and only ~8% couples into the air blast (`air_coupling`), so the bunker sees ~1 psi at 12 km while the 3.7 km dust column fills a meaningful part of the view. Every recommended distance is checked by the selftest (bunker overpressure < 2 psi, thermal fluence below the 2nd-degree burn threshold).
- **D012 (stage 6):** The default humidity is 0.35 (desert test range). Wilson condensation clouds need humid air, so they appear on the Pacific/tropical shots (Baker, Ivy King, Ivy Mike, Castle Bravo use `humid_site`) or when the player raises the humidity (H key).
- **D013 (stage 7):** The range-objects module (`scripts/world/range_objects.gd`, built by a module agent and verified separately) is set up from the same `ExplosionProfile` as the explosion: yield, height of burst, surface flag, fireball radius, crater radius and the air-blast coupling of buried/underwater shots (`blast_yield()/w_kt`), so the destruction order matches the bunker overpressure exactly. It is excluded from Baker's lagoon.
- **D014 (stage 10):** `RenderingServer.global_shader_parameter_get()` is editor-only in Godot 4.7 (prints an error and returns null in a running game). The auto-quality controller therefore keeps its own copy of the `g_quality` value instead of reading the global back. Found by the QA matrix.
- **D015 (stage 10):** The QA driver installs a Godot `Logger` (`OS.add_logger`, available since 4.5) and counts every engine/script/shader error per launch, so "no errors in the log" is asserted inside the run, not only by grepping stdout afterwards. Range objects are excluded from the node-leak count because their number legitimately depends on the bomb.
- **D016 (stage 10):** Air-burst clouds get a "wake chimney": from the moment the head rises clear of the burst point a thin smoky column trails beneath it (as in the Hiroshima, Nagasaki, RDS-37 and Tsar photographs), and the ground dust column climbs up into it later with a ragged front. Before this the cap of a high burst floated free above a short dust stem, which no reference shows. `stem_detached` keeps the old behaviour for shots where a gap is documented.
- **D017 (stage 10):** The 3D noise volumes are no longer `FastNoiseLite.get_seamless_image_3d()` output: its blended seam skirt showed up as an axis-aligned "burlap" grid on large fireballs (review cycle 2). `tools/gen_noise.py` builds exactly periodic gradient-noise fBm and Worley-billow volumes (integer lattices that wrap at 96 voxels) once with numpy and stores them as `data/noise_fbm.bin` / `data/noise_billow.bin` (0.9 MB each); `FxResources` loads them in a few ms and falls back to FastNoiseLite if they are missing. Fireball octaves are also sampled through a fixed rotation so lattice directions never line up.
- **D018 (stage 10):** Wind drift: the stem and ground skirt shear with height, but the cloud head, fireball, rings and ice caps translate RIGIDLY with the wind at the head's height. Shearing the noise domain inside a compact head tilted puffs into "rugby balls" and smeared the noise into hair.
- **D019 (stage 10):** Fireball, head, stem top, rings and camera framing share one centre (`ExplosionProfile.head_center_eff`), which blends the tanh rise into the measured cloud-top keyframes. Before this the ball could hang 1-3 km below its own cloud. Megaton fireballs hand over to the cloud between explicit per-bomb windows (`fb_fade_s`, `head_appear_s`) taken from the films instead of fixed multiples of t2.
- **D020 (stage 10):** Review renders and screenshots use one fixed binocular field of view per phase (fireball / growing cloud / mature cloud), fitted to the largest extent in that phase and measured from the cloud's near edge, so scale growth is readable and 100 km caps are not clipped. Each bomb's site humidity (`site_humidity`: Pacific shots 0.85, Hiroshima 0.8, Nevada 0.25) is the default until the player changes humidity.
- **D021 (stage 10):** Ground heave (Sedan's 115 m dome, the camouflet bulges) is drawn as an opaque, cracking soil cap inside the explosion volume in addition to the terrain displacement, because the far terrain LOD cannot resolve a 100 m feature 12 km away.
- **D022 (stage 10, cycle 3):** The 'pom-pom / felt / hairball' look of every cloud head (all three review cycles) had one root cause: the head noise lookups were ~3x too high-frequency for the noise volumes (the billow volume holds 6/12/24 Worley cells per tile, so `nbl(np*0.32)` gave lobes of ~0.12 R and `np*0.95` ~0.04 R). Rescaled to big lobes of ~0.35 R that carry their own sub-lobes (the cauliflower hierarchy); the fine octaves now only shade.
- **D023 (stage 10, cycle 3):** Per-bomb tuning in cycle 3 runs as 5 parallel technical-artist agents. To avoid conflicting edits, each owns one override file `tools/profiles_c3/group_[A-E].py` (`CYCLE3 = {bomb_id: {...}}`, merged last by `bomb_profiles.py`), the lead owns all code, and `gen_bombs.py` writes `data/bombs.json` atomically (`os.replace`) so a concurrently starting Godot instance never reads a half-written file. Code changes the artists need are returned as requests and integrated by the lead.
- **D024 (stage 10, cycle 3):** Review framing (`shot_rig.gd`): one fixed field of view per growth phase with phase cuts so no phase spans much more than ~6x growth (extra cut at 0.03 t_stab; the heave/vent of buried shots gets its own phase; `frame_phase_s` overrides), ground zero 22 % above the bottom edge (its true elevation below the eye's horizontal is taken into account), rings/ice caps/dome included in the fitted extent. The binocular view is capped at 58 deg (wider, the embrasure lintel enters the frame); the outside 'near' camera widens up to 100 deg.
- **D025 (stage 10):** Bunker interior materials ignore the sky ambient light (`disable_ambient_light`): Godot has no occlusion for sky ambient indoors, so at dawn the whole concrete ceiling glowed orange. The interior is lit by its own lamps, the flash and the window. The consoles got procedural monochrome CRT displays (text rows, a trace graph, scanlines) instead of flat green rectangles.
- **D026 (stage 10):** Baker's lagoon was never visible: the polar water disc in `water_pond.gd` is wound counter-clockwise and Godot treats clockwise faces as front faces, so `cull_back` removed it. The water shader now uses `cull_disabled`.
- **D027 (stage 10):** A fifth time of day, PREDAWN (sun 3 deg below the horizon, warm horizon band, faint stars, clouds lit from the bright eastern sky), is used as the historical setting for Trinity (05:29 MWT, before sunrise). It is not in the T-key cycle or the menu (those keep the four presets of the task), so the UI is unchanged.
- **D028 (stage 10):** HE (conventional) bursts barely change a camera's exposure in daylight: the metering stops down at most 0.25 stop, and the nuclear-style aureole/sky flash (`g_fireball_color`, `g_flash`) is scaled by 0.12/0.2 for HE. The HE flame sits on the ground (45 % of the squashed ellipsoid below the terrain), has a hot core and a cooler orange rim, and fades into soot below ~1800 K instead of rendering as red paint.
- **D029 (stage 10):** Condensation rings are thin laminar sheets: the noise frays the rim and thins the sheet instead of displacing the thickness isotropically (which made 1-2 km 'cotton planks'), they are lit fully by the fireball, and they are never placed inside or right under the cloud head. The free-air shock-front condensation band is shown only for seconds near the strong front (p > 4-10 psi, t < 12 s).
- **D030 (stage 10):** New explicit art keys replace unit traps and hard-coded windows: `wilson_dur_s` (seconds; `wilson_duration` is a multiplier of the physics duration, and King's 5.0 had produced a 48 s opaque shell), `wilson_hum_min`, `stem_fade_s`, `skirt_fade_s`, `fb_cool_s`, `fb_grow_s`, `skin_start`, `head_appear_rel`, `torus_x0/torus_dx`, `cap_aspect_keys`, `col_top_keys`, `jet_fade_s`, `head_lobes`, `stem_top_flare`, `whiteout_s`, `near_m`, `frame_phase_s`.
- **D031 (audio rework):** The explosion sound is synthesized per event (bomb, ground range, time of day, humidity) instead of being assembled only from generic pitched layers: no sample library can be both licence-clean and right for 20 bombs at 0.5-150 km. The synthesis runs once per launch on the `WorkerThreadPool` (1.5-6 s) while the booster flies (always > 10 s), so it never blocks a frame; `sound_manager` falls back to the generic layers when the result is not ready at the blast arrival or the key (bomb, range, time of day, humidity) does not match. The front is rendered at 32 kHz (the shock edge needs the bandwidth), the tail at 8 kHz (after 8+ km of absorption nothing above ~2 kHz is left), which keeps memory and CPU small.
- **D032 (audio rework):** Levels: the front's peak follows the physical peak SPL through a compressive map (100 dB -> -30 dBFS, 140 -> -10, 160 -> -4, >= 180 -> -1) and the tail plays at its physical ratio to the front. Raw physics would put a Mk 82 at 2 km 30 dB under Tsar Bomba at 150 km; loudspeakers and ears cannot carry that, but the order and the character (sharp crack vs. long dull roll) are kept. The 'body' of a nuclear report (a 55 -> 32 Hz thump of min(t+, 0.5 s)) is added on top of the infrasonic N-wave, because a 5-20 s positive phase is inaudible on loudspeakers - the same trick film sound uses.
- **D033 (audio rework):** The atmosphere is a preset chosen from the time of day: noon = lapse (shadow zone beyond sqrt(2 * 86 km * H) + 0.6 km: duller, later, -12 dB at most), dawn/predawn/night = ground inversion (no shadow; 1-2 extra arrivals 1.5-6 s later), dusk = neutral. Profiles may force it with `sound_atmos` (RDS-37 uses "dawn": the 1955 shot was fired at 09:47 local in November with a strong inversion; the shock 'reflected' from the inversion injured people far away).
- **D034 (audio rework):** Mountain echoes are smeared (diffuse reflection over 0.5-2 s, 0.6 s decay) and filtered by air absorption over their own path, so a 30-70 km echo is a low thud, not a bright copy of the bang; the reverberant field between echoes decays with a release of 2.5-8 s (longer far away) above a coda bed with T60 = 3 + 2 log10(W + 1) + R_km / 20 s. All absorbed kernels are tapered at both ends (the zero-phase FFT filter spreads the pulse both ways; truncating it clicked in every echo).
- **D035 (audio rework):** Rocket crackle is a Poisson train (300/s) of steepened shocklets with Pareto(1.3) strengths, tuned offline in numpy against the published statistics of solid-rocket noise (waveform skewness ~0.4, derivative skewness >= 5; in-game loop 0.29 / 4.3 after the loop crossfade). Soft clipping would lower the crest factor but destroys the skewness that makes crackle audible, so the loop is left unclipped (crest factor ~12) and the rocket player is 6 dB louder instead.
- **D036 (audio rework):** The falling bomb is not a cartoon whistle: a broadband aerodynamic rush (wake band ~0.2 V/d, fin hiss 3-12 kHz) with a faint fin resonance, inaudible beyond ~5 km slant range and silent under a retarding parachute (MOAB, FOAB, B83, RDS-37, Test 219, Tsar Bomba) - eyewitnesses of Hiroshima and Nagasaki heard no incoming bomb at all.
