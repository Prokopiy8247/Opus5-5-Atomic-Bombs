# CREDITS

## Software
- Engine: **Godot Engine 4.7.2-stable** (MIT License) — https://godotengine.org
- No third-party addons, GDExtensions, models, textures or fonts are used. All geometry, textures (noise) and shaders are generated procedurally in code; the explosion and rocket sounds are recordings generated for this project with ElevenLabs (see Sound), everything else in the audio is synthesized in code.
- `data/noise_fbm.bin` and `data/noise_billow.bin` are tileable noise volumes generated by this project's own `tools/gen_noise.py` (deterministic, no external data).
- Fonts: the operating system's monospace fonts via `SystemFont` (Consolas / Lucida Console / Courier New); not redistributed.

## Sound
- **Explosion and rocket recordings** (`assets/audio/samples/`): generated by the project owner with **ElevenLabs Sound Effects** (model v3, https://elevenlabs.io) from the prompts in `docs/ELEVENLABS_PROMPTS.md` (one per bomb, plus the megaton rumble bed, the bunker window rattle, the rocket ignition and the rocket flight roar), on the owner's ElevenLabs account; use is governed by the ElevenLabs terms for that account. They were cut, levelled and split by `tools/process_samples.py` (the raw downloads stay outside the repository in the local folder `Звуки взрывов/`). The rocket ignition adds a synthesized ignition-overpressure pulse to the recorded roar.
- Everything else is synthesized at runtime (`scripts/audio/`): ground tremor, debris, glass, falling-bomb rush, tinnitus, UI sounds, the bunker ventilation and hum (both switched off, D040), and the per-event blast synthesis that remains as the fallback for an event without a recording. No sample libraries were downloaded.
- The acoustics behind the prompts and the in-game adaptation (distance, shadow zone, inversions, air absorption, rumble balance) come from published sources summarised in `docs/RESEARCH_NOTES.md` section 7 (Glasstone & Dolan, DTIC/NASA reports, KNMI's 1922 Oldebroek survey, arXiv papers on long-range blast propagation, BYU rocket-noise and crackle research, ISO 9613-1, Atomic Heritage Foundation eyewitness documents, A. Wellerstein's notes on dubbed test films).

## Data sources
Historical and scientific data come from public sources listed in `docs/RESEARCH_NOTES.md` (Glasstone & Dolan *The Effects of Nuclear Weapons*, Fletcher 1963, DNA/DTRA reports, LANL/LLNL/DOE/NNSA publications, Sublette's Nuclear Weapons Archive, NUKEMAP methodology, Wikipedia and others). No weapon-design information is included.
